MazeTrap privacy policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

MazeTrap is an offline-first game. Gameplay, progression, local leaderboards, sound, and the tutorial work without an internet connection.

Optional online services

MazeTrap uses internet access only for anonymous gameplay analytics and the optional global leaderboard.

Anonymous analytics are enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings. They may include an app-generated random identifier, game version, coarse operating-system and device class, screens viewed, tutorial progress, level attempts and outcomes, elapsed time, moves, pushes, invalid moves, pauses, and control method. When an analytics batch reaches the service, the hosting edge may infer a two-letter country code from the request's network address. The network address itself is not retained by MazeTrap. MazeTrap does not collect names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, contacts, precise location, or move-by-move traces for analytics.

Global leaderboard participation is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings without affecting offline gameplay or local scores. MazeTrap suggests a local display name using Android's permission-free device-name setting when available, or the device model as a fallback. The initial global display name follows that local name, but you can edit it independently or restore the local name in Settings. Submitted data includes an app-generated random player identifier, the global display name, level, completion time, move count, game and rules versions, a scenario fingerprint, and an integrity token on supported Google Play builds. The hosting edge may also infer a two-letter country code for the submitted score. A small country flag or code may be displayed publicly with that score. Country inference can be inaccurate when a relay, mobile carrier, or VPN is used.

Purpose and retention

Analytics are used to understand reliability, tutorial effectiveness, game difficulty, aggregate usage, and broad country-level differences. Raw analytics, including the inferred country code, are retained for up to 90 days. Daily aggregates by country may be retained for up to 24 months. Reports should suppress very small country cohorts.

Leaderboard data is used to display daily, weekly, monthly, and all-time rankings and to prevent abuse. Accepted all-time scores may be retained while the global leaderboard operates, including the country code associated with each score. Expired shorter-period ranking rows may be removed after an operational grace period.

Storage, transfer, and service providers

Queued online data is stored on your device until it can be sent. Automatic traffic is limited to 1 MiB of application payload per UTC day. Production transfers use HTTPS.

MazeTrap's hosting and database providers process data only to operate these services. Data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Country codes are derived by the hosting edge during a service request. The app does not request Android location permission and does not access GPS, Wi-Fi positioning, or precise location.

Your controls

In Settings you can turn analytics off. Turning analytics off deletes queued analytics and resets the random analytics identifier. You can leave the global leaderboard without affecting offline gameplay or local scores. Global leaderboard participation includes public display of the selected global display name and the score's inferred country flag or code when available. Local display names remain on the device. Clearing the app's storage removes locally stored MazeTrap identifiers and queued data.

Display names that violate leaderboard rules may be rejected. Scores or players may be removed or blocked to protect the leaderboard.

Children

MazeTrap is not designed to collect personal information from children. The online services use random identifiers and do not request an email address, real name, precise location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the online services change. The effective date above will be updated. For privacy or deletion requests, email info.relit.studio@gmail.com and include the leaderboard display name and player identifier shown by the app when available.
